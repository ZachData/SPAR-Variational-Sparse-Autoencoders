# Confirmatory battery — results, 2026-09-03

Companion to `FINDINGS_2026-09-02.md` (what was wrong with the pilot) and
`REMEDIATION.md` (what was done about it). This file records **what the corrected
battery measured**.

Design: 6 seeds per arm on gelu-1l, `d=2048`, `k=256`, layer 3 residual stream,
10k steps. Liveness measured over 1,000,192 streamed samples per checkpoint.
Every p-value below is `0.00216`, which is the **exact two-sided minimum for 6v6** —
it is the design floor, not a measure of effect size. Read the `d` column.

---

## E1 — is a fixed-variance vSAE the same thing as TopK + an L2 penalty?

CLAUDE.md landmine 1 says yes, as algebra: at `sigma = 1` the KL reduces to
`0.5*||mu||^2`, which is exactly what `activation_penalty` computes. That algebra
was verified directly — both trainers return `511.895264` on the same batch, to six
decimals. The empirical question is whether the trained *checkpoints* match.

Two confounds were removed in sequence. Their effect on the measured difference:

| metric | kl_warmup unmatched | + bias form unmatched | **both matched** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `frac_variance_explained` | −58.9 | −50.2 | **−6.6** |
| `frac_recovered` | −43.8 | −39.0 | **−9.7** |
| `frac_alive` | +37.1 | +6.2 | **0.0** |

**`frac_alive` is now exactly identical** — 1.000000 ± 0.0000 in both arms, p = 1.0.
The pilot's headline E1 difference was, on that construct, entirely an artifact of
two implementations being compared as though they were two loss functions.

### But the liveness metric says the arms are still not the same

The pre-registered live-feature metric is the sparsity-relative one (F8b), reported
at **both** thresholds, with the rule fixed in advance: *robust only if both agree;
a disagreement is itself the finding.* Here they disagree — **in direction**:

| threshold | `e1_penalty` | `e1_vsae_ref` | d |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 0.1 × (k/d) | 0.0007 ± 0.0003 | 0.0018 ± 0.0006 | **−2.5** |
| < 0.5 × (k/d) | 0.1846 ± 0.0109 | 0.0928 ± 0.0022 | **+14.0** |

At the tighter cut the vSAE has *more* near-dead features; at the looser cut it has
*half as many*. A pure location shift cannot do that. **The two arms' feature-usage
distributions differ in shape, not merely in position** — which is exactly the case
the two-threshold pre-registration exists to expose, and which a single threshold
would have reported as a clean one-directional result in whichever direction that
threshold happened to favour.

### Verdict on E1, stated carefully

* **As algebra: confirmed.** The objectives are numerically identical.
* **As a claim about checkpoints: not established.** Reconstruction still differs by
  ~0.5% (d ≈ 7–10) and the usage distribution differs in shape (d = 14 at 0.5×).
* **A third unmatched difference is already identified:** `threshold_start_step` is
  500 for `e1_penalty` and 1000 for `e1_vsae_ref`. (`warmup_steps` reads as
  `None` vs `200` in the saved configs but is *not* a confound — `train_topk.py`
  resolves `None` to `max(200, 0.02*10000) = 200`, the same value.)
* The arms also come from two different training scripts, so trainer-internal
  differences beyond the config surface are still possible.

**The equivalence-margin problem is now the binding issue, not statistical power.**
Across-seed SDs are ~0.0008, so a difference of 0.0055 FVE lands at d ≈ 7 and reads
as "significant" at the floor. E1 is an *equivalence* claim and `PROJECT.md` still
lists its margin as an open decision. Without a margin in scientific units, this
test will reject equivalence for differences far too small to matter — and the
margin cannot be chosen now that these numbers have been seen.

## E3 — the ReLU is not a nuisance, it is a large effect

`vsae_topk.py` applies `F.relu(mu)`; the masked-KL trainer did not; the preprint's
equations show none. Rather than declare either correct, both were run (F9b).

| threshold | `e3_masked_kl` (no ReLU, preprint) | `e3_masked_kl_relu` (ReLU, released code) | d |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 0.1 × (k/d) | 0.1309 ± 0.0060 | 0.0298 ± 0.0038 | +20.8 |
| < 0.5 × (k/d) | 0.4654 ± 0.0060 | 0.3134 ± 0.0093 | +19.8 |

Both thresholds agree in direction and magnitude, so this result **is** robust under
the pre-registered rule. Adding the ReLU cuts the near-dead population by a factor
of ~4.4 at the tight threshold.

**This retrospectively justifies not resolving the preprint/code disagreement by
fiat.** Had one trainer simply been patched to match the other, every E3 number
would have silently inherited a d ≈ 20 effect attributed to the KL mask. The
preprint's equations and the released code describe materially different models,
and any claim about "the masked-KL vSAE" must say which one it means.

## E2 — it is the sampling, not the KL

### Stage 1: no beta in the grid gives a working variational SAE

One seed per beta at `var_flag=1`. Baseline mean FVE 0.900335; the rule wanted the
largest beta within 0.02 of it.

| beta | 1e-4 | 1e-3 | 1e-2 | 1e-1 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FVE | 0.4581 | 0.2843 | 0.1367 | 0.0004 | 0.0001 |
| qualifies | no | no | no | no | no |

Nothing qualified; the rule took its fallback and selected 1e-4. The column of noes
is the real output: E2 was designed on the hypothesis that beta = 1.0 was simply too
large once sampling was on, and that a smaller beta would recover a healthy model.
It does not.

### The control: beta = 0 with sampling still on

The pilot cannot distinguish "the KL destroys reconstruction" from "the
reparameterisation does". `kl_coeff = 0, var_flag = 1` is the one configuration that
separates them — sampling on, KL contributing exactly zero. Six seeds.

| config | FVE |
|---|---|
| baseline (deterministic) | 0.900335 ± 0.0004 |
| `var_flag=1`, beta=1e-4 (KL on) | 0.459302 ± 0.0026 |
| `var_flag=1`, **beta=0 (KL off)** | 0.482791 ± 0.0025 |

    total gap to baseline        0.4410
    recovered by removing the KL 0.0235   ( 5.3% of the gap)
    attributable to the SAMPLING 0.4175   (94.7% of the gap)

**Turning the KL entirely off recovers 5.3% of the damage.** The remaining 94.7% is
the reparameterisation. Liveness agrees and is robust across both thresholds:
baseline vs beta=0 sampling is d = +144.9 (0.1×) and +56.9 (0.5×), while beta=1e-4
vs beta=0 is only d = +7.5 and +3.7.

This is a **claim about the architecture, not about a hyperparameter**, and it is
considerably stronger than the beta-tuning story E2 was originally built on. It also
means the degeneracy narrative and the collapse narrative are about different
things: the fixed-variance vSAE (E1) is a deterministic model and reconstructs fine;
the moment sampling is switched on, reconstruction halves regardless of beta.

### Caveats that stand

* `e2_confirm` characterises a model at FVE ≈ 0.46, not a healthy one. Liveness on
  it carries a reconstruction-collapse caveat and belongs in
  `confounders_uncontrolled`, as recorded **before** these runs were read.
* `e2_sampling_only` is **not pre-registered** — proposed and run after stage 1 was
  seen. It is a control rather than a selection step, so it does not contaminate
  `e2_confirm`, but it is exploratory and is reported as such.
* `e2_learned_var` (beta=1.0) remains the cautionary case: FVE 0.000076,
  `frac_recovered` −0.736, and yet `frac_alive` = 1.0000 and `features_used` =
  2047.8/2048. A destroyed model reads as fully alive on the metrics the preprint
  used. The sparsity-relative metric catches it (0.998 near-dead at 0.1×).

## What is not yet done

1. **E1's equivalence margin** — the binding open decision. Needs a value in
   scientific units, chosen on what difference would matter, not on these numbers.
2. **`threshold_start_step`** — match at 500 or 1000 and re-run E1 (~12 runs, ~12 min
   training + ~14 min analysis) to see whether the residual d ≈ 7–14 survives.
3. **E4** — the size-matched SCR/TPP control. No training required; still needs the
   `scorer(keep_indices)` function described in RUNBOOK section 1.
4. **Extending the E2 beta grid downward** (1e-5, 1e-6). The control makes this much
   less interesting than it looked — if 94.7% of the damage is the sampling, a
   smaller beta cannot recover a healthy model — but it would close the question
   formally. It would be a new pre-registration, not a continuation of this one.
